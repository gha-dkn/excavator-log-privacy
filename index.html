Privacy Policy

Last updated: 07-29-2026

This privacy policy describes how Excavator Log ("the app") collects, uses, and protects information. This app is an internal tool built for a single business's job and expense tracking, used by a small, fixed group of authorized users (business owner/admin, business partners, a fuel supply contractor, and a driver). It is not available to the general public.

Who operates this app

Ghafoor Amerizada
Contact: ghafoor.amerizada@gmail.com

Information we collect

Account information

  • Name, phone number, and email address used to sign in
  • User role (admin, partner, fuel contractor, driver)

Job and business records

  • Job locations, descriptions, dates, and completion status
  • Fuel delivery records, including quantity, cost, and notes
  • Expense records (parts, repairs, maintenance, and related costs)
  • Payment records, including amounts and dates (payments themselves are made in cash outside the app; the app only stores a record for reporting purposes)

Photos

  • Receipt photos attached to fuel deliveries and expenses, uploaded by the user submitting the record

Device and technical information

  • A push notification token, used solely to deliver in-app notifications (e.g. payment confirmations, job reminders)
  • Basic app usage data necessary for the app to function offline and sync data when a connection is available

SMS-based status updates (optional feature)

  • If a user sends a text message to report job status while offline, that message is received on a personal phone operated by the app administrator and relayed to the app to update the relevant job record. A confirmation reply is sent back to the sender.

How we use this information

Information collected is used only to operate the app's core functionality:

  • Displaying jobs, fuel logs, expenses, and payments to authorized users based on their role
  • Sending push notifications for payments, job reminders, and rate change proposals
  • Processing SMS status updates, where used
  • Generating summary reports and exports for the business's own records

We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. We do not use this data to serve ads.

Third-party services

The app relies on the following infrastructure providers to function:

  • Google Firebase (database, authentication, file storage, and cloud functions) — data is stored on Firebase's servers
  • Expo (push notification delivery)

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy practices. We do not control their infrastructure directly but select providers that offer industry-standard security practices.

The optional SMS status-update feature does not route through a third-party SMS provider; it operates through a personal mobile phone and number belonging to the app administrator.

Data access and roles

Access to information within the app is restricted by role:

  • Admin and business partners can view all job, fuel, expense, and payment records
  • The fuel contractor can view only their own fuel delivery and payment records
  • The driver can view only their own assigned jobs and payment history

Data retention

Records are retained for as long as the app is in active use for the business's recordkeeping needs. A user may request deletion of their personal account information by contacting ghafoor.amerizada@gmail.com; note that financial and job records tied to their account may be retained as part of the business's ongoing records unless removal is legally required.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, including role-based access controls enforced at the database level, and reliance on providers (Firebase, Expo) that encrypt data in transit and at rest. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same location with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact
